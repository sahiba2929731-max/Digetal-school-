REAL DIGITAL SCHOOL (RDS) — STARTER DEMO

Included:
- index.html: standalone Hindi school prototype.
- README.txt: setup and limitations.

How to test on Android:
1. Extract this ZIP using your phone's Files/File Manager app.
2. Open the extracted RDS_Real_Digital_School folder.
3. Tap index.html and choose a browser if asked.
4. If your phone does not open local HTML, upload the folder contents to a static hosting service.

Important:
- This is a FRONTEND DEMO only.
- AI teacher replies are a small offline demo, not a connected AI model.
- Login, shared student accounts, live chat/video, secure database, real payments and server-verified unlocks are NOT implemented.
- The diamond buttons are demo-only. Do not send money.
- Do not use for real children or official school records until backend security, guardian consent, privacy controls, curriculum review, moderation, backups and payment verification are implemented.
